Changes made for this prototype:
- Re-encoded from 1280 x 534 to 854 x 356, H.264 (CRF 27), 24 fps, AAC 96 kbps stereo.
- One keyframe every 24 frames (1 second) and fast-start metadata, so seeking is quick.
- Extracted one 160 x 90 thumbnail per second (letterboxed) and packed them into
  10 x 10 JPEG sprites: tos-thumb-01.jpg to tos-thumb-08.jpg (the last sheet holds 35).
- Full timeline kept: 734.17 seconds (12 min 14 s). Not cut, looped or stretched.
