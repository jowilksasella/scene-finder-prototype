Local modifications for the exploratory comparison prototype:
- Re-encoded the full 854 x 480 video as H.264, yuv420p, 24 fps, CRF 23.
- Removed audio; kept the complete original visual timeline.
- Added regular 24-frame / 1-second keyframes and MP4 fast-start metadata for seeking.
- Extracted one 160 x 90 thumbnail at each integer second from 0 to 237.
- Packed thumbnails in row-major 10-column x 10-row JPEG sprites.
- Last sprite contains 38 real thumbnails; the remaining 62 cells are blank.

Measured duration (ffprobe): 237.916667 seconds (approximately 3 min 58 sec).
This is a true short film, not a 60-minute film. It has not been looped or stretched.
It is only a sample for testing the candidate interactions; formal coursework material
and the final interaction design have not been selected by the user.
